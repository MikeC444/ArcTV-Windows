# Release notes

User-facing changes to Arc TV for Windows, newest first. This file is the release notes shown in the app's own update pop-up: whatever sits under a
version heading becomes that release's description. Add each change as a bullet under `## Unreleased` as you make it; publishing a release files them
under its version automatically.

## Unreleased
- Clicking a title in Continue Watching starts the recommended source straight away, from where you left off.
- Settings > Addons shows your addons two to a row.
- More space between the rows on Home, and bigger titles under the posters.
- The source list no longer has a Copy link button.
- Resizing the window while a video plays is smooth now: the controls step aside while you drag and come back when you stop. They also follow the window when you move it.
- The episode line in the player shows the season and episode once, even for titles saved earlier with it twice.
- Continue Watching now starts the same source you used last time, or shows the list when it cannot be sure which one that was, instead of starting a different one that might not play.
- The top bar is bigger: a larger logo, bigger menu buttons and a bigger profile button.
- A slow or unreachable server no longer closes the app.
- The player's controls now sit at the very bottom of the picture on every screen, and clicking the Audio, Subtitles or Speed button again closes its list.
- A source that does not start no longer leaves the player loading for ever: after a while it says so, and a new Sources button in the player (and on that message) takes you to the list to pick another.
- The title's logo shows while a video loads (and on the source list and in the player) even when you start it from Continue Watching.
- The player has bigger controls: a larger pause / play button, bigger back and 10-second skip buttons, and a much bigger title logo. Everything along the bottom sits lower, and the volume bar is no longer cut off. Pausing or playing shows its symbol in the middle of the picture for a moment.
- Resuming an episode no longer shows its number twice.
- Profiles (Arc TV Plus): "Who's watching?" appears when you sign in, with pictures to choose from and a PIN for profiles that have one. Manage Profiles adds, edits and removes them, and "Switch profile" in the account menu comes back to it. Each profile keeps its own list, ratings, Continue Watching and settings, and a kids profile hides mature genres.
- The app no longer starts over by itself when your sign-in is renewed in the background.
- Play carries on with the source you used last time, without asking again. A new "Sources" button on a title lets you pick a different one.
- Smart source picking (Arc TV Plus) starts the best source that can play, once every addon has answered.
- The next episode starts by itself when one ends (if Auto Play Next Episode is on), and a Next episode button shows in the last minute.
- Episodes show a tick when watched and a bar for how far you got. Click the tick to mark one watched, or use Mark season watched. Play starts the next episode you have not seen.
- Section headings on a title's page (Seasons, Cast, You May Also Like) are bigger.
- The top of a title's page is bigger: the name or logo, the details, the description and the buttons.
- "You May Also Like" now always sits at the bottom of a title's page.
- Like and Not for me on a title are now a thumbs up and a thumbs down.
- The arrows that scroll a row sideways are bigger.
- Row titles on Home are bigger and easier to read.
- Posters are bigger, so fewer fit across the screen at once.
- Sign in with your email, or scan a QR code with your phone. You stay signed in next time.
- Home is here: a rotating banner (with More info and My List buttons) and rows of movies and shows from your addons.
- Movies and TV Shows pages: pick a genre, sort by Featured, Highest Rated or Newest, and keep scrolling for more. Posters are bigger and fade in, scrolling glides, and rows spread to fill the window as you resize it.
- Search is here: type to search every addon at once, pick from suggestions, and get your recent searches back.
- My List is here: your saved titles, with a green tick on the ones you've watched, and sorting.
- Click any poster to open its page: artwork, description, seasons and episodes, cast, and a Trailer button, release date, cast photos, similar titles, and buttons to add it to My List or mark it watched. Press Play to see every source your addons offer, best quality first . Press Back, Esc or your mouse's back button to return.
- Playing is here: choose a source and the video plays inside Arc TV, with the Arc TV logo top right, a loading screen with the artwork and a spinner, play/pause, skip 10 seconds, a timeline, volume, audio and subtitle choices, speed, and full screen (F, or double-click). Direct links play now; torrents are coming.
- Settings is here, laid out like the web app: Account (sign out, refresh Home), Addons, Home Rows (switch rows on or off and move them), Blocked Genres (hidden everywhere), Player, Subtitles (on or off, size, colour with a preview, language), Audio language, and Updates (check for a new version).
- Settings > Addons is here: see the addons on your account, switch each on or off, remove one, or add one by pasting its link (Torrentio, for example). Home, Movies and TV Shows update straight away.
- The Plus pages are here: Arc TV Plus (what you get, your plan, cancel, and subscribe on Stripe's secure page), Plus settings, Recommendations (what you've liked or marked Not for me, and what shapes your picks) and Your stats (hours watched, streak, a 13-week map and your busiest day).
- Like and Not for me on a title's page (with Arc TV Plus): rate a movie or show from Windows. What you rate shows under Settings > Recommendations and syncs to your other devices.
- Continue Watching is here: Home has a row of what you were watching, with how far in you got, and a click starts it from where you stopped. Play on a title says Resume when you've left it part-way. Arc TV tells your account where you are every half minute while you watch, so it follows you to your other devices. A movie watched to the end gets its green tick on My List.
- Picked for you is here (with Arc TV Plus): a Home row chosen from the movies and shows you like, finish and save, with the reason under each poster ("Because you liked ..."). A cross takes a title out for 5 days. Until you've rated or saved a few titles Home shows popular ones instead, clearly labelled.
